Privacy Policy

About Help & contact Privacy policy

Applies to the app “Solitaire adfree 4ever” for iPhone and iPad. Last updated: 28 October 2026.

Short version: this app collects nothing.

What the app does not do

  • It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code.
  • It never connects to the internet. It works fully offline.
  • It does not ask for, collect, store or share any personal information: no name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or usage data.
  • There are no accounts and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your game in progress, your settings and your statistics are saved only on your own device, so that you can carry on where you left off. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in your device’s Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send the developer anonymous crash reports. This is entirely under your control in the Settings app and can be switched off at any time.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page before the app changes.
