Free forever. No ads. No subscriptions. No tracking. No internet connection needed.

A calm, classic game of Solitaire for iPad and iPhone, made for parents and grandparents who simply want to play without being interrupted by advertisements — and for everyone else, too.

What you get

  • Big, clear cards, with a large-print option and four-colour suits
  • Tap a card and it moves to the right place; dragging works too
  • Unlimited hints and unlimited undo, always free
  • Deals that can always be won, and a friendly notice if no winning moves are left
  • Turn over 1 card (easier) or 3 cards (classic)
  • Deal of the Day: the same deal for everyone, so the whole family can compare
  • Also FreeCell and Spider
  • Works in portrait and landscape, in 11 languages, with VoiceOver
